ABOUT MY WORK

People. Water.
A changing planet.

I want to understand the dynamic feedbacks between human activities and the hydrologic cycle—and what they mean for the places we live.

How do changes in land use and water management reshape environmental conditions? How do changes in water availability, quality, and risk influence our choices? These questions connect my interests in human impacts, ecological resilience, and adaptation.

My undergraduate work on coastal ecological resilience and land-use change examined how human pressures relate to ecosystem conditions. At ASU, my ongoing ADEQ project with Ruijie Zeng focuses on identifying potentially unpermitted on-site wastewater facilities in Arizona. Across these settings, I use spatial evidence to examine the relationship between people and their environment.
