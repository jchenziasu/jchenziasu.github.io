Published article 01

Published2024 · Land

Assessment and Prediction of Coastal Ecological Resilience Based on the Pressure–State–Response (PSR) Model

Zhaoyi Wan, Chengyi Zhao, Jianting Zhu, Xiaofei Ma, Jiangzi Chen, Junhao Wang

Land, 13(12), 2130

Research overview

The study applies the Pressure–State–Response framework to assess ecological resilience at the pixel scale across China’s coastal zone. It examines spatial and temporal changes and compares projected resilience under different climate and development scenarios.

My contribution

Data analysis. I am a coauthor of the published article.

The related project was supervised by Chengyi Zhao at Nanjing University of Information Science and Technology.

Impact of Spartina Alterniflora Expansion on the Tidal Creek Formation in the Mudflat of Yellow Sea

Research focus
